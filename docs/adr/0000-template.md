## Context

What situation forces a decision? The constraints, the problem, and the relevant
facts. Keep it factual — no marketing.

## Decision

What we decided, stated plainly. If there are several parts, use a numbered list,
each with a short *Why:* justifying it on technical merits.

## Consequences

What follows from this — the trade-offs accepted, what gets easier or harder, and
any follow-up work or new constraints. Note anything a future reader would
otherwise be surprised by.
